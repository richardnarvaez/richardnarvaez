## What it is

Watch a short clip, hide or show the captions, then recall the words from natural speech. Progress and stories live in Convex; sign-in goes through Clerk.

## Why a prototype

It is the web sibling of the iOS app in Work. The interesting parts for the Lab are the learning loop as a state machine and how progress syncs across devices in real time.
