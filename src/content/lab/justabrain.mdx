## What it is

An infinite canvas where notes, images and ideas live wherever you drop them. Pan, zoom, cluster. The layout is the thinking.

## Why a prototype

It mixes both sides of the Lab: the system design of a canvas that stays fast with thousands of items, and the small algorithmic exercises inside it, hit testing, culling, spatial indexing.
