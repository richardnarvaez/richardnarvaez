## What it is

A draggable globe with a marker per place. Pick a country to fly there; pick a marker to open its stamp: where, when, one line about it.

## Why a prototype

Rendering a globe smoothly on a phone is a system design problem in miniature: what to draw, when, and how to keep the frame budget. The camera easing and the marker projection are the exercises.
