## What it will show

Move the pointer across a strip. Three rows draw the events: the raw stream, the debounced output, and the throttled one. Sliders change the wait time and switch leading and trailing edges.

## Why

Both words get used for each other in code review. Seeing the two rows side by side settles it faster than a definition.
