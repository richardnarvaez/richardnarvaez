## The product

One of my first apps on Google Play, with several versions, and one of the most popular I have ever shipped. It did five things well:

- Music download
- Offline player
- Automatic cover art lookup
- Metadata search
- Lyrics

## Google banned me

Read the policies. I was young and built the app people wanted, but it broke some Google Play rules. I did not measure that risk, and Google closed my developer account for good. A hard hit while I was still learning.

<Callout>
  The lesson stuck: a product that lives on someone else's platform lives by their rules. Every project since starts by reading them.
</Callout>

## What it left

Good earnings and a bigger lesson. After this I moved into the web: frontend, backend, servers. With Google Play closed to me, mobile had to wait a while.
